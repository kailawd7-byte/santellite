Understanding what is happening in your body:

In microgravity, body fluids shift toward the chest and head because gravity no longer pulls them downward. The heart doesn't have to work as hard to supply the brain, leading to cardiovascular deconditioning, cardiac atrophy, and a risk of orthostatic intolerance or syncope during posture changes or return to Earth.

Recommended Operational Countermeasure

Maintain 45 minutes of daily cardiovascular exercise (T2 treadmill with bungee harness or CEVIS cycle ergometer).
